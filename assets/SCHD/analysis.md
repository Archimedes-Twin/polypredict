## Performance (6 months)

SCHD has risen in a persistent uptrend, climbing from a trough in the middle of the window to a recent peak. The latest close sits just below that high, in the upper part of its range. The most recent stretch was the strongest of the window, an acceleration after a steadier climb. That late burst leaves the fund extended, so a brief pause or shallow pullback would not damage the picture. The overall shape is orderly and higher, with no sign of a broken trend.

## Drivers

The central driver is a hawkish Fed. Markets price a further hike as nearly certain, which keeps real yields elevated and inflation concerns alive. Higher real yields pressure long-duration, richly valued growth and favor cash-generating dividend payers. That suits this basket's tilt toward staples, healthcare and energy. Firm crude supports the energy weight and the inflation-sensitive makeup of the sleeve. Geopolitical risk matters mainly through oil, where the market is close to undecided on whether crude breaks lower. That is the main macro uncertainty and the one leg the rotation evidence does not address.

## Continue or revert

Continue. The market odds already sit at a very high level, and the evidence-adjusted posterior moves only slightly above them, so the video evidence adds conviction rather than changing direction. There is no real conflict.

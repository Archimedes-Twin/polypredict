## Performance (6 months)

SCHD has risen in a steady, mostly orderly uptrend, from a spring trough in early May to a peak in early November. The latest close sits just under that high, so the fund trades near the top of its range rather than bouncing off the bottom. The most recent month was the strongest stretch of the window, a sharp acceleration after a slower grind higher. The drawdown from the peak is shallow, and the path looks like persistent accumulation rather than a spike followed by a retreat.

## Drivers

The main driver is rotation out of AI-heavy growth and into the sleeves SCHD owns: consumer staples, healthcare, energy and utilities. October's sector tape showed all four leading, which is the clearest evidence for the call. Adam Khoo's framework says the rotation arrives in stages, out of AI, back in, then out again over a long stretch, so value catch-up should repeat rather than end after one burst. In July he was already buying profitable non-AI dividend compounders almost daily in anticipation of this move, and the tape has since borne that out. On policy, the market prices a high chance of a Fed hike at the December meeting. That signals a hawkish, inflation-sensitive backdrop in which cash-generative, lower-multiple businesses are favored over long-duration growth. It also keeps rate risk on the table for any bond-proxy dividend holdings.

## Continue or revert

The call is continuation, and the rating stays very bullish. The evidence-adjusted posterior sits somewhat above the raw market prior, and the two point the same way. The odds expect the trend to persist, and the rotation evidence adds a fundamental reason for it, so there is no conflict to resolve. The modest lift over the prior reflects staged rotation and sector leadership that the odds alone do not capture. The main risk is the sharp last month, which leaves the fund extended. A pullback or a temporary swing back into AI would fit the staged-rotation thesis and would not break it. The call would change if hike odds fade materially below the level where the signal is flagged for revision, which would undercut the hawkish backdrop supporting the value rotation. It would also change if sector leadership clearly handed back to AI-led growth and held there, rather than in a brief round trip.

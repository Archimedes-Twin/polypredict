## Performance (6 months)

SCHD has moved in a persistent, orderly uptrend, climbing from a trough in the middle of the window to a recent peak. The latest close sits just below that high, in the upper part of its range. The most recent stretch was the strongest of the window, an acceleration after a steadier climb. That late burst leaves the fund extended, so a brief pause or shallow pullback would not damage the picture. There is no sign of a broken trend.

## Drivers

The central driver is a hawkish Fed. Markets treat a December hike as nearly certain, which keeps real yields elevated and inflation worries alive. Higher real yields weigh on long-duration, richly valued growth and favor cash-generating dividend payers with sturdy balance sheets, which suits this basket's tilt toward staples, healthcare and energy. Firm energy prices support the energy weight. Softer enthusiasm for AI-linked tech demand pushes capital toward profitable value names. Easing expectations are the open question, since a cut by next summer is priced as close to a coin flip.

## Continue or revert

Continue, in line with the very bullish rating. The market odds on the hike are the strongest input, and the posterior sits somewhat below them. The cut-timing signal is roughly neutral and holds back conviction. Khoo's rotation thesis agrees with the hawkish picture rather than contradicting it.

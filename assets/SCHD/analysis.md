## Performance (6 months)

SCHD has climbed in a persistent uptrend, from a midsummer trough to a peak in early November. The latest close sits just below that high, in the upper part of its range. The most recent month was the strongest stretch of the window, a clear acceleration after a steadier climb. That late burst leaves the fund extended, so a short pause or shallow pullback would not change the picture.

## Drivers

The main driver is a hawkish Fed. The market treats a hike at the December meeting as very likely. A tightening path pressures long-duration, richly valued growth and favors cash-generating dividend payers, which suits this basket's tilt toward staples, healthcare and energy. Firm crude adds support through the energy weight and the inflation-sensitive makeup of the sleeve.

The rotation evidence points the same way. Adam Khoo describes October leadership in staples, healthcare, energy and utilities, which is the SCHD sleeve. He argues that money leaves AI in stages, so value catch-up should repeat rather than end, and he is buying profitable non-AI dividend compounders on that view. These items all come from a single source and their relevance fades with time. The signal would be revised if hike pricing faded materially, and a sharp slide in crude would remove part of the basket's support. The oil leg is the only loose end: the WTI sub-$90 bet is not a clean read, and Khoo does not address it, so I keep it out of the evidence.

## Continue or revert

The market odds, the realized trend and the rotation evidence all agree, so there is no conflict to resolve. I weight the market odds as the anchor because they are explicit, falsifiable and tied directly to the macro regime that favors this basket. I treat the rotation commentary as a corroborating nudge, since it comes from a single narrative source whose weight decays. The posterior stays very bullish with high confidence, and I favor continuation over reversion.

The main risk is not a thesis break but the fund's stretched position after a strong month, which invites consolidation. The call weakens if hike expectations fade, if crude rolls over hard, or if leadership swings back toward AI growth. A deeper decline from the early-November high would suggest accumulation is turning into distribution.

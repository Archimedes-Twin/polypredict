## Performance (6 months)

SCHD climbed out of a clear mid-year trough and finished the window with a modest net gain. The advance was a steady, orderly grind higher, not a sharp spike, and it peaked in early November. The deepest drawdown was moderate and came in the earlier part of the window, and the fund fully recovered from it. The latest month has slipped slightly, so the fund sits a touch below its high. That looks like a shallow pause after a long climb, not a break in the trend. Overall the picture is a gradual uptrend with little choppiness and one minor recent wobble.

## Drivers

The main driver is a hawkish Fed. A December hike is priced as close to certain, which keeps real yields elevated and inflation worries alive. That backdrop tends to weigh on long-duration growth and favors cash-generating dividend payers with sturdy balance sheets, which suits SCHD's tilt toward staples, healthcare, energy and utilities. Sector rotation adds to this. October's tape showed money moving out of AI leaders and into the dividend and value sleeves that make up the fund. The commentary behind that call describes the rotation as staged over one to two years, with swings out of AI, back in, and out again, so the value catch-up should repeat rather than end in one move. One investor is also steadily accumulating profitable non-AI dividend compounders in anticipation of an anti-bubble rotation. The risks are a surprise dovish turn from the Fed that revives growth leadership, and a sharp slowdown in labor or earnings that hurts the cyclical parts of the basket, such as energy.

## Continue or revert

The market prior and the video evidence point the same way, so there is no conflict to resolve. The crowd is strongly bullish and expects the uptrend to continue. The evidence, though older and discounted for age, supports that view: the rotation inflow, its staged and repeating nature, and the steady buying of non-AI dividend names all favor a continued catch-up for the basket. The posterior therefore sits slightly above the prior, consistent with a very bullish read. The recent slip below the high is best seen as consolidation inside a rotation that still has room to run. The call would weaken if the market's pricing of a December hike fell well away from near-certainty, since the signal revises if hike odds drop below the 45% threshold. It would also weaken if the rotation out of AI reversed decisively, with growth leadership retaking the tape and the fund losing its mid-year recovery base near the $31 area. Absent those, continuation is the more likely path.

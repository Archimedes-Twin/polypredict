## Performance (6 months)

SCHD has risen in a persistent uptrend, from a midsummer trough to a peak in early November. The latest close sits just below that high, so the fund trades in the upper part of its range. The most recent month was the strongest stretch of the window, a clear acceleration after a steadier climb. That late burst leaves the fund extended, so a short pause or shallow pullback would not change the picture.

## Drivers

The main driver is a hawkish Fed. Markets price a hike at the December meeting as very likely. A tightening path pressures long-duration, richly valued growth and favors cash-generating dividend payers, which is how this energy, healthcare and staples-heavy basket tends to do well. Firm crude adds to that support through the energy weight and the inflation-sensitive tilt of the sleeve.

The video evidence points the same way. Adam Khoo describes October leadership in staples, healthcare, energy and utilities, which is the SCHD sleeve, and argues that rotation out of AI proceeds in stages, so value catch-up should repeat rather than end. His buying of profitable non-AI dividend compounders fits that view. These items all come from one source and their relevance fades with time. The view would be invalidated if hike pricing fades materially, which would revise the signal. A sharp slide in crude would also remove part of the basket's support.

## Continue or revert

The market odds and the realized trend agree, and the video evidence adds support in the same direction. There is no real conflict to resolve. I weight the market odds as the anchor because they are explicit, falsifiable and tied directly to the macro regime that favors this basket. I treat the video evidence as a corroborating nudge, since it is a single narrative source whose weight decays. The evidence-adjusted view therefore stays strongly bullish, and I favor continuation over reversion.

The main risk is not a thesis break but the fund's stretched position after a strong month, which invites consolidation. The call weakens if hike expectations drop, if crude rolls over hard, or if leadership swings back toward AI growth. A deeper decline from the early-November high would suggest accumulation is turning into distribution.

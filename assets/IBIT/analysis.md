## Performance (6 months)

Over the past six months IBIT chopped sideways inside a very wide range and finished with a modest net loss. It bottomed at the end of June, then rallied hard into a late-September high, and has since given back a large share of that advance. The most recent month has been the sharpest leg down, leaving a deep drawdown from the peak. Price now sits in the upper-middle of the half-year range rather than at either extreme. The shape reads as a failed breakout, not a clean uptrend or downtrend.

## Drivers

The Fed path is the dominant macro driver. The market prices a high likelihood of a rate hike at the December meeting, which keeps real yields and financial conditions restrictive. That is a poor backdrop for a liquidity-sensitive asset with no earnings or cash flow. The one evidence item, Adam Khoo's early-July remark that he may agree with Grantham that Bitcoin goes to zero, leans the same way. It frames the asset as a speculative, mania-chasing vehicle with nothing to anchor valuation, so there is no earnings or cash-flow support to offset tighter policy. Because the comment is old, its decayed weight is small, and the hike pricing carries most of the case. Energy and geopolitics are not flagged as active inputs here.

## Continue or revert

The call is continuation lower, matching a very bearish rating with high confidence. The evidence-augmented posterior is essentially identical to the raw market prior, so the evidence does not fight the odds. The only evidence item points the same direction as the hike pricing and adds little beyond it. The crowd's expectation of continued weakness agrees with the six-month trend, and the failed breakout and sharp recent leg down fit that read. A rebound from here would be a counter-trend bounce, not a reversal. What would change the call is a collapse in Fed hike expectations, specifically hike odds for the December meeting falling well below even, which would remove the main restrictive-policy pressure and justify revising toward neutral. Until then, the hawkish Fed path and the absence of fundamental support favor further downside over a reversion higher.

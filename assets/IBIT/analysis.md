## Performance (6 months)

IBIT swung through a wide range and ended with a modest net loss. It bottomed, rallied hard into a late-period high, then gave back a meaningful part of that advance. The most recent leg down was the sharpest. Price sits in the upper part of its range rather than at either extreme, so the shape reads as a failed breakout, not a clean trend.

## Drivers

The Fed path is the dominant macro driver. Markets price a very high likelihood of a hike at the year-end meeting, which keeps real yields elevated and financial conditions restrictive. That is a poor backdrop for a liquidity-sensitive asset with no earnings or cash flow to anchor its valuation. Sticky inflation and yield pressure sit behind the hike pricing and carry the macro regime through to the asset.

The video evidence leans the same way. Adam Khoo said he may agree with Grantham that Bitcoin goes to zero, and he treats it as a mania-chasing vehicle. The remark is old and its weight has faded, so it adds a skeptical voice but little new information.

## Continue or revert

Continue: the very bearish rating stands. The evidence-adjusted posterior is essentially unchanged from the market-implied starting point, because the video item agrees with the odds and adds almost nothing. There is no conflict to resolve, and I trust the market pricing because it carries the weight here. The call reverts only if hike odds fall under 45%. Until the Fed's tightening path softens, weakness is more likely to persist than fade.

## Performance (6 months)

Over the last six months IBIT chopped through a very wide range and ended with a modest net loss. It bottomed at the end of June, rallied hard into a late-September high, and has since given back a large share of that advance. The latest month has been the sharpest leg down, which leaves a deep drawdown from the peak. Price now sits in the upper-middle of the half-year range rather than at either extreme. The shape reads as a failed breakout, not a clean trend in either direction.

## Drivers

The Fed path is the dominant macro driver. The market prices a high likelihood of a rate hike at the December meeting, which keeps real yields and financial conditions restrictive. That is a poor backdrop for a liquidity-sensitive asset with no earnings or cash flow to anchor valuation. The one evidence item points the same way. In early July Adam Khoo said he may agree with Grantham that Bitcoin goes to zero, and he treats it as a speculative, mania-chasing vehicle. The item is older and carries decayed weight, but it adds a valuation-skeptic voice to a rate backdrop that is already hostile. Inflation and yield pressure, which sit behind the hike pricing, are the channel through which the macro environment weighs on the asset.

## Continue or revert

The call is bearish: continuation of the downtrend over reversion. The evidence-augmented posterior is essentially identical to the raw market prior, so the Khoo remark confirms the crowd's pricing and does not fight it. The odds, the recent price action and the single evidence item all lean down, which leaves no conflict to resolve and supports high conviction. The rebound from the June low was real but has largely unwound, which fits a failed breakout inside a restrictive-rate regime. The main thing that would change the view is a repricing of the Fed. If December hike odds fell well below the midpoint, the rate headwind would ease and the signal would be revised toward neutral or better. A sustained recovery through the September high would also show that buyers are overriding the macro backdrop. Absent either, the path of least resistance stays lower.

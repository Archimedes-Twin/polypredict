## Performance (6 months)

IBIT swung through a wide range and ended with a net loss. It bottomed, rallied hard into a late-period high, then gave back a meaningful part of that advance. The most recent leg down was the sharpest. Price sits in the upper part of its range rather than at either extreme, so the shape reads as a failed breakout inside a broader downtrend, not a clean collapse.

## Drivers

The Fed path is the dominant macro driver. Markets price a near-certain hike at the year-end meeting, which keeps real yields elevated and financial conditions restrictive. That is a poor backdrop for a liquidity-sensitive asset with no earnings or cash flow to anchor its valuation. Sticky inflation, supported by firm energy prices, sits behind the hike pricing. The market is split on whether cuts arrive by mid-next-year, so it offers only a mild offset. Adam Khoo's remark that he may agree with Grantham that Bitcoin is headed to worthlessness adds a skeptical voice, but its weight has decayed.

## Continue or revert

Continue: the very bearish rating stands. The video evidence points the same way as the market odds, so there is no conflict to resolve. I lean on the market pricing because it carries far more weight than a single fading opinion. The split cut-timing odds soften the picture slightly but do not overturn the hike signal. The call revises only if hike odds fall below their invalidation threshold, meaning the tightening path has genuinely softened. Until then, further weakness is more likely than a durable recovery.

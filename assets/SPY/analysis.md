## Performance (6 months)
Over the past six months the index moved sideways in a broad range. It bottomed in early summer, climbed to a peak in early October, and has since pulled back modestly. It still sits in the upper part of that range with a small net gain.

## Drivers
The main macro driver is the earnings cycle. Khoo cites aggregate profit growth that beat expectations, broadening beyond the largest tech names, with higher margins attributed to AI cost savings. Forward valuation is below its five- and ten-year averages because earnings outgrew price, and long yields sit near historical norms. On geopolitics, the Iran ceasefire-hold odds are low, which is the risk factor. Seasonal post-midterm patterns and a record hedge-fund de-grossing are historical patterns Khoo cites, not guarantees.

## Continue or revert
Judgment: bullish. The raw market-prior view was bearish, driven by the low odds that the US-Iran ceasefire holds. The earnings breadth, the below-average multiple, forced-deleveraging washouts that historically preceded lows, and the post-midterm seasonal tendency outweigh that single geopolitical proxy over this horizon. Khoo's floor call of a modest further dip is an opinion, not a fact. The view would be revisited if ceasefire-hold odds recover above the revision threshold shown on this page, which would change the geopolitical read, or if earnings breadth deteriorates.

## Performance (6 months)

Gold has had a hard half-year. It peaked early in the window, then slid in a persistent, orderly way to a trough. The drawdown was deep but gradual, not a crash. The bounce off the low has been shallow, and the price still sits nearer the bottom of its range than the top. The latest month was flat to slightly softer, and no clear base has formed yet.

## Drivers

The main headwind is the Fed path. The market treats a hike at the year-end meeting as the overwhelmingly likely outcome. That keeps real yields firm and raises the opportunity cost of holding a non-yielding asset. A hawkish Fed also tends to support the dollar, which is a further drag on gold. This rate channel is the only live signal behind the call.

Geopolitics and oil are the main offsets. Renewed conflict could revive safe-haven demand and energy-driven inflation worries. For now that is not feeding into Fed expectations, so it does not outweigh the rate channel.

## Continue or revert

Continue: the call stays very bearish. No video or other evidence is on file, so nothing conflicts with the market odds and there is nothing to resolve. The evidence-adjusted posterior simply echoes the odds, and I trust them because they are the only live input and they carry a clear mechanism for gold through real yields and the dollar. The call reverts if hike odds fall under 45%, which would remove the rate headwind. Until then the bias is lower.

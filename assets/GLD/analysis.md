## Performance (6 months)

Gold has had a hard stretch. It peaked early in the window, then slid in a persistent, orderly way to a trough. The drawdown was deep but gradual, not a crash. The bounce off the low has been shallow, and the price still sits nearer the bottom of its range than the top. The latest month was flat to slightly softer, and no clear base has formed yet.

## Drivers

The main headwind is the Fed path. The market treats a hike at the year-end meeting as the overwhelmingly likely outcome. That keeps real yields firm and raises the opportunity cost of holding a non-yielding asset. A hawkish Fed also tends to support the dollar, which weighs further on gold.

The second signal, a Fed cut by mid-next-year, is close to a coin flip. It hints at easing later but does little to relieve near-term pressure. Energy-driven inflation worries and geopolitics are possible offsets, but they are not yet shifting Fed expectations.

## Continue or revert

Continue: the call stays very bearish. No video evidence is on file, so nothing conflicts with the market odds and there is nothing to resolve. I lean on the odds because the hike signal is decisive and works through real yields and the dollar. The undecided cut signal is a mild offset that tempers conviction, so confidence is medium rather than high. The call would revert if hike odds fell below the invalidation threshold, removing the rate headwind. The cut-timing signal has its own threshold, and a sharp loss of belief in easing would reinforce the bearish case rather than overturn it.

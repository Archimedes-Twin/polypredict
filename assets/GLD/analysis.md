## Performance (6 months)

Gold has had a hard half-year. It peaked in late spring and then slid in a persistent, orderly way to a trough at the very end of October. The drawdown was deep but gradual, not a crash. The bounce since the low has been shallow, and the price still sits closer to the bottom of its range than the top. The latest month was flat to slightly softer, so the direction remains down and no clear base has formed.

## Drivers

The main headwind is the Fed path. The market treats a hike at the final meeting of the year as the likelier outcome. That keeps real yields firm and raises the opportunity cost of holding a non-yielding asset, which is the clearest macro explanation for the slide from the spring high. It is also the only signal behind this call.

No video evidence is on file for gold, so the commentary layer neither backs nor challenges the rate story. The main offset is geopolitics. Renewed conflict could revive safe-haven demand and energy-driven inflation worries, but that is not currently feeding into expectations for the Fed.

## Continue or revert

The evidence-adjusted view has nothing to work with beyond the market odds, so it simply echoes them. The crowd expects continuation and the half-year price trend agrees, which leaves no conflict to resolve. I weight the market odds because they are the only live input, and they point the same way as price action.

The call stays very bearish with high confidence, and the slide is more likely to continue than revert while a hike remains the base case. The view would be revisited if hike odds fell decisively below even, which would ease the pressure from real yields and allow a rebound from depressed levels. A sharp geopolitical shock that overrides rate expectations would be a second reason to reassess. Without either, the downtrend and the pricing agree.

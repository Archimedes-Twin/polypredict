## Performance (6 months)

Gold has had a hard half-year. It peaked early in the window, then slid in a persistent, orderly way to a trough. The drawdown was deep but gradual, not a crash. The bounce off the low has been shallow, and the price still sits nearer the bottom of its range than the top. The latest month was flat to slightly softer, and no clear base has formed yet.

## Drivers

The main headwind is the Fed path. The market treats a hike at the year-end meeting as the overwhelmingly likely outcome. That keeps real yields firm and raises the opportunity cost of holding a non-yielding asset. A hawkish Fed also tends to support the dollar, which is a further drag on gold.

A second signal, whether the Fed cuts by mid-next-year, is close to a coin flip. It hints at easing later but does little to relieve near-term pressure. Geopolitics and energy-driven inflation worries are possible offsets, but they are not yet shifting Fed expectations.

## Continue or revert

Continue: the call stays very bearish. No video or other evidence is on file, so nothing conflicts with the market odds and there is nothing to resolve. I lean on the odds because the hike signal is decisive and has a clear mechanism for gold through real yields and the dollar. The undecided cut signal is a mild offset that only tempers conviction, which is why confidence is medium rather than high. The call reverts if hike odds fall below the invalidation threshold, which would remove the rate headwind. Until then the bias is lower.

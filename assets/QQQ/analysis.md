## Performance (6 months)

QQQ has trended lower. Summer strength, when the short- and medium-term trends still lined up, failed to hold. Rallies were sold and the index rolled over into autumn. The latest leg was a sharp, policy-triggered one-day drop on top of a slow grind lower in the AI leaders. The fund sits well below its highs with no convincing base, and bounces have been shallow. The overall shape is a failed advance giving way to orderly selling, then forced selling, as crowded positions were cut.

## Drivers

The Fed is the main driver. A hike at the final meeting of the year is priced as nearly certain, so inflation has not been tamed. Higher real yields cut the present value of distant earnings, which hits a long-duration, growth-heavy index hardest. Energy costs keep inflation sticky. Easing is priced only as a distant, near coin-flip prospect.

Tech demand is the offset. Hyperscaler revenue is growing, but heavy capex has pushed free cash flow negative. That makes the AI buildout more sensitive to credit and financing conditions. Data-center moratorium rhetoric added a policy shock, and rotation into defensives continues.

## Continue or revert

Continue lower. The market gives a rise lower odds than a decline, and the posterior stays bearish at medium confidence. The video evidence lifts it from the prior but not enough to flip it.

The videos conflict with the market. They lean bullish: policy-shock rebound, intact earnings, a young AI cycle and modest valuations. I lean toward the market. Those arguments are mostly long-horizon or sentiment-based, and some are aging. The hike pricing acts directly on multiples now. A sharp retreat in hike odds would invalidate this call.

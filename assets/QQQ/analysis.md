## Performance (6 months)

QQQ has trended lower over the window. Late-summer strength, when the medium-term trend still looked aligned upward, did not hold. Rallies were sold, and the index rolled over into autumn. The latest leg was a sharp, policy-triggered one-day drop on top of a slow grind in the AI leaders. The fund now sits well below its highs, with no clear base and only shallow bounces. The pattern looks like a failed advance giving way to orderly, then forced, selling, and momentum still points down.

## Drivers

The Fed is the dominant driver. Markets price a year-end hike as near certain, which means inflation has not been tamed. Higher real yields cut the present value of distant earnings, and that hits a long-duration, growth-heavy index hardest. Easing is priced only as a distant, roughly even-odds prospect, so relief is not near.

Tech demand is the offset. Hyperscaler revenue growth is solid, but heavy capex has pushed free cash flow negative. That makes the buildout more sensitive to financing costs and credit conditions. Data-center moratorium rhetoric added a policy shock. Crowded AI longs are also being unwound as money rotates into defensives.

## Continue or revert

Continue. The market gives a rise low odds. After weighing the video evidence, the posterior improves only slightly and stays firmly in very bearish territory.

The videos lean bullish. They argue for a quick V-shaped rebound, intact earnings, a young AI cycle and low valuations compared with the dot-com era. That conflicts with the odds. I lean toward the odds. They aggregate capital that is pricing the discount rate, which is the force compressing multiples now. The videos are one commentator's view, and their rebound analogies come from calmer rate regimes. Valuation arguments also work over years, not this horizon.

The call would revise only if hike odds collapsed from near-certainty to clearly doubtful.

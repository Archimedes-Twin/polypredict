## Performance (6 months)

The index finished the six months roughly flat after a wide round trip. It troughed in late July, rallied to a peak in early October, then sold off sharply in the latest month. That left a sizable drawdown from the high.

## Drivers

Rates are the main macro driver. Futures price a high chance of a Fed hike by the December meeting, alongside only a coin-flip chance of cuts by mid next year, which pressures long-duration growth multiples. The earnings cycle is a counterweight: hyperscaler revenue growth is strong, and Khoo attributes negative free cash flow to growth capex and one-time items rather than weaker operating cash flow. Rotation out of crowded AI positions and hedge-fund de-grossing weigh on near-term flows.

## Continue or revert

Judgment: neutral. The raw market-prior view was bearish because of the hawkish Fed odds. We soften it to neutral because the fundamental evidence, intact hyperscaler earnings and valuations well below dot-com levels, offsets the rate headwind. Khoo's dot-com analogy is a historical pattern and a weak signal for a short horizon. The view would turn more cautious if hike odds stay elevated while the crowding unwind continues. It would improve if hike odds fall below the revision threshold shown on this page or cut-by-July-2027 odds fall below the revision threshold shown on this page, which would revise the rates read.

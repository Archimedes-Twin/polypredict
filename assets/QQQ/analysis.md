## Performance (6 months)

Over the six months QQQ ended modestly lower after a wide round trip rather than a clean trend. It troughed in late summer, rallied into a peak in early October, then gave back the gains in a sharp slide through early November. The latest month did most of the damage: a steep, fast drawdown that took the fund to its low of the window on the final observation. The path was a failed breakout followed by a fast unwind, not a slow grind lower.

## Drivers

Rates are the main macro driver. Futures price a high chance of a Fed hike by the December 2026 meeting, while the chance of cuts by the July 2027 meeting is only about even. That combination keeps pressure on the long-duration growth multiples that dominate the index. Earnings partly offset this. Khoo reads the hyperscalers' negative free cash flow as growth capex and one-offs such as the EU fine and TPU inventory, not weaker operating cash flow, and revenue growth at Google, Meta and Amazon remains strong. Positioning is the other force. AI names are well off their highs, money is rotating in stages toward financials, healthcare and staples, and crowded hedge-fund longs are being degrossed, which argues for near-term underperformance of the AI-heavy index. His valuation comparisons with the dot-com era (a younger boom, far smaller gains, much lower forward multiples) point to long-run upside but say little about the next three months. His earlier warning that overpriced pure-AI infrastructure stocks fall hardest at cycle end adds downside skew.

## Continue or revert

The call is neutral with low confidence. The raw market prior is also neutral but sits a bit lower, reflecting hawkish Fed pricing. The evidence lifts the posterior only slightly above that prior, because the fundamental and valuation points (intact hyperscaler cash generation, an early-stage boom) outweigh the rotation and degrossing signals just enough to offset the rate headwind, though not enough to turn bullish. The two sides conflict. The odds say rates will stay restrictive, and the flow evidence says the selloff has not finished. Against that, fundamentals are intact and the drawdown is already deep, so neither continued decline nor a clean reversion has a clear edge. We treat the drift as undecided. The call turns bearish if hike odds for December stay high and cut odds for mid-2027 fall below the invalidation line, since that would confirm a lasting restrictive path. It turns constructive if hike odds fall below their invalidation level and the cut path firms, easing the pressure on growth multiples while hyperscaler operating cash flow holds.

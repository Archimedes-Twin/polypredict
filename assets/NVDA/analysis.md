## Performance (6 months)

The shape has been choppy rather than smooth. A leverage-driven AI-complex drawdown in midsummer was followed by a sharp recovery when a blowout earnings report forced a re-rating higher. The most recent leg is a fresh air pocket on leadership headlines. The path looks like repeated shakeouts inside a still-constructive range, not a clean climb or a broken chart.

## Drivers

Tech demand is the anchor. Hyperscaler capex, cloud growth and foundry guidance point to compute demand that is still expanding. Credit and positioning matter more for timing, because the summer drop looked like forced deleveraging and not a demand shock. Sticky inflation and a cautious Fed keep discount rates elevated, which pressures high-multiple growth. Data-center power costs are a background cost factor. Custom chips from large cloud customers are a slower moat risk.

## Continue or revert

The judgment is bullish, with low confidence. The posterior sits above the neutral prior, so the evidence tilts upward, but only modestly. The video evidence conflicts with the market on one point. It cites prediction-market pricing of a likely CEO removal, a bearish event signal. I lean bullish because that overhang is a fast-fading, headline-driven risk, while the demand, moat and valuation evidence decays slowly. I would revert if margins weaken, revenue decelerates, or hyperscaler spending is cut.

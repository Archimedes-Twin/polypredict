## Performance (6 months)

No reliable six-month performance data is available, so only a brief qualitative note is possible. The shape of the recent move cannot be characterized.

## Drivers

The main macro driver is the discount-rate argument: with long yields elevated, companies that still lose money and have distant cash flows face valuation pressure. This is a valuation-framework opinion, not a forecast of a specific outcome. It is partly offset by structural demand for network and security infrastructure, so the pressures roughly balance over three months. A decline in long yields would ease the headwind.

## Continue or revert

Neutral with low conviction. The rate-sensitivity concern is a real headwind for unprofitable growth, but it is a single opinion-based source and there is no price data to show it is being realized. The offsetting structural demand keeps the balance even, so no directional stance is warranted. A sustained further rise in long yields would push toward a more cautious view.

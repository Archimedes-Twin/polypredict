## Continue or revert

The posterior is neutral with low confidence, leaning only slightly toward upside. There is no raw market prior to compare against, since Polymarket supplies nothing for this asset, so the call rests on the evidence alone and no conflict between odds and evidence needs resolving. The Khoo endorsement and the rotation-toward-profitable-compounders thesis justify a small positive tilt, but one commentary item without price confirmation or a priced macro signal is too thin to call a continuation of strength or a reversion. The call stays neutral. It would turn constructive if the rotation into non-AI earners broadened and showed up in market-based signals, and it would turn negative if the AI-led leadership reasserted itself and pulled capital away from defensive compounders, or if the insurer's earnings quality visibly deteriorated. Until one of those appears, treat the stance as a weak lean, not a conviction.

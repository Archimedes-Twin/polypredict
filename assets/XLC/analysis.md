## Performance (6 months)

The communication services sector has trended lower over six months. It peaked in early May and slid to a low at the end of October, with a small bounce since. It was the worst sector in October, leaving a deep drawdown.

## Drivers

The earnings cycle and rates are the main drivers. Heavy AI capex has triggered sentiment-driven de-rating at the largest constituents, even though Khoo argues revenue and operating income are growing and the cash-flow dip reflects fines and inventory timing. Elevated Fed hike odds raise discount rates on long-duration equities. Khoo also flags structural risk that consumer AI agents disintermediate ad-funded platforms, which is a predictive opinion, not an established fact.

## Continue or revert

Judgment: very bearish. The raw market-prior view was also very bearish, and the evidence confirms it over this horizon. Rotation outflow, capex-sentiment pressure, hawkish rate odds and the agentic-disruption narrative all point down. Khoo's argument that the fundamentals are intact is a real counterweight and could mean a sharp rebound, which is why this is a short-horizon call. The view would be revisited if hike odds fall below the revision threshold shown on this page.

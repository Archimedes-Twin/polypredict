## Drivers

Video evidence mostly reinforces it: communication services was October's worst sector (-12.25%, rotation outflow, LR 1.20 at age 1) and the structural Oct-14 leg — consumer AI agents disintermediating ad-funded search/marketplaces (Meta's News/Muse app #1 on both US stores; display ads lose value when agents, not humans, browse) — adds a down-direction claim at LR 1.20 with a 180-day half-life (decayed 1.18 at 20 days). The offsetting up-leg is real: hyperscaler fundamentals intact — GOOGL rev +25%/op income +30%, META +11% ex-fine (LR 1.28 decayed 1.27 at 4 days) — but it pays out on a quarters horizon. Prior 27.5 → posterior 25.4: the evidence made it worse, not better.

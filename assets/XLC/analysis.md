## Performance (6 months)

XLC has trended lower across the window. The decline looks like persistent distribution rather than a single shock. It intensified most recently, when communication services was the weakest sector in the market and Meta and Alphabet fell far below their highs. Nothing in the pattern suggests a durable recovery has begun, and the fund still sits well under its earlier peak.

## Drivers

The Fed is the central driver. The market prices a hike, not a cut, at the final meeting of the year. A higher policy path lifts real yields and discount rates on long-duration growth equities, and mega-cap platforms dominate this sector. Sticky inflation, possibly reinforced by energy costs, keeps policy tight. Tighter credit raises the bar for heavy capex programs. AI-related capex is growth spending, but markets punish it before the payoff shows. Agentic disintermediation of ad-funded platforms is a slower structural headwind.

## Continue or revert

Continue lower. The market odds agree with the trend, and the posterior is very bearish with high confidence. The video evidence conflicts. It argues the selloff is sentiment, that capex will convert to earnings, and that cash drags are temporary. I lean toward the odds, because the rate path sets the multiple on these stocks now, while fundamental repair runs on a slower clock. The structural ad-erosion evidence points the same way. The call would revise if hike odds fell below the invalidation threshold.

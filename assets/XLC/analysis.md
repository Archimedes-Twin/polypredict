## Performance (6 months)

XLC has trended lower across the window. The decline looks like steady distribution rather than a single shock. It worsened most recently, when communication services was the weakest sector in the market and Meta and Alphabet fell far below their highs on capex worries. The fund still sits well under its earlier peak, and the pattern shows no sign that a durable recovery has begun.

## Drivers

The Fed is the central driver. Markets price a hike, not a cut, at the final meeting of the year. A higher policy path lifts real yields and discount rates on long-duration growth equities, and a few mega-cap platforms dominate this sector. Sticky inflation, possibly reinforced by energy costs, keeps policy tight. Tighter credit raises the bar for heavy capex programs. AI infrastructure demand is real, but markets penalize the spending before the payoff arrives. Agentic disintermediation of ad-funded platforms is a slower structural headwind.

## Continue or revert

Continue lower, in line with the very bearish, high-confidence posterior. The market odds agree with the trend. One video conflicts with them. It argues the selloff is sentiment rather than fundamentals, that growth capex will convert to earnings, and that the cash drags are temporary. I lean toward the odds. The rate path sets the multiple on these stocks in the current window, while fundamental repair runs on a slower clock. The other two video claims, on outflow and ad erosion, point the same way as the odds. The bearish call would be invalidated if hike odds fell well below the threshold the signal names.

## Performance (6 months)

XLC has trended lower across the window. It peaked early on and then slid in a mostly steady grind, with no sustained recovery attempt. The decline sharpened late in the period, when communication services was the weakest sector in the market, and the low arrived in the final stretch of that selloff.

The bounce off the low has been shallow, and the most recent month still closed in the red. The fund sits well below its high. The drawdown looks like persistent distribution rather than a single shock.

## Drivers

The Fed path is the central macro driver. The market prices a hike, not a cut, at the final meeting of the year. A higher policy path lifts real yields and discount rates on long-duration growth equities, and mega-cap platforms dominate this sector. Inflation that keeps policy tight leaves little cushion.

Rotation outflows and capex-sentiment fear in Meta and Alphabet added sector-specific pressure. The counterargument is that this is growth capex, with strong earnings and one-off cash drags. That supports a sentiment-driven drop, but it plays out over quarters. Agentic AI disintermediation of ad-funded platforms is a slower structural headwind.

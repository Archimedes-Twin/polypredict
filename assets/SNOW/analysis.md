## Drivers

The key macro driver is the discount-rate argument: with long yields elevated, a company whose cash flows lie far in the future sees its intrinsic value cut more than profitable peers. This is a valuation-framework opinion rather than a verified outcome. Any easing in long yields or continued enterprise data-infrastructure demand would partly counter it, leaving the balance roughly even over a three-month horizon.

## Continue or revert

Neutral with low conviction. The rate-sensitivity headwind is real in principle, but it rests on one opinion-based source and there is no price evidence that it is being realized. Offsetting demand for data infrastructure prevents a negative stance. A further sustained rise in long yields would push toward a more cautious view.

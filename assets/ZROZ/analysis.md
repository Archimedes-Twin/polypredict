## Performance (6 months)

Long-duration Treasuries fell over six months, from a June peak to a trough in early October. The most recent month brought a sharp rebound, but the fund is still well below its high with a deep drawdown.

## Drivers

The key macro driver is inflation. Khoo's framework says long yields are anchored to inflation plus real growth, so a hot-inflation regime pushes long rates up regardless of Fed policy. That is his model, not a settled fact. Against it, only roughly even odds of Fed cuts by mid next year limit the case for a sustained bond rally. Fed policy controls the short end, so the long end depends on inflation expectations.

## Continue or revert

Judgment: neutral. The raw market-prior view was also neutral, and the evidence confirms it. The inflation-anchored upward drift in long yields offsets the recent rebound and the possibility of eventual cuts. The view would be revisited if cut-by-July-2027 odds fall below the revision threshold shown on this page, which would strengthen the bearish case. A sustained inflation decline would argue for a more constructive stance.

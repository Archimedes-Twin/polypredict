## Continue or revert

The call is very bullish, with low confidence, and it leans toward continuation of the recovery rather than reversion to further weakness. There is no raw market prior to compare against, so no conflict between odds and evidence needs resolving. The posterior comes entirely from fundamentals and valuation. Revenue and cash flow are growing, the legal overhang is cleared, and the price sits below conservative intrinsic value. Together these point to a sentiment-driven selloff that should fade. Confidence stays low because the evidence is a single commentator's valuation work and the market adds no independent check. The call would weaken if the earnings engine cracked, for example through slowing revenue or cash flow ex the fine. It would also weaken if capex growth outran cash generation, if agent-app adoption stalled, or if a new legal or regulatory liability appeared. A further de-rating without any fundamental improvement would suggest the sentiment drag is more durable than Khoo assumes.

The call is bullish with low confidence. There is no raw market prior for GOOGL, so the posterior comes entirely from the evidence, and no market odds are available to confirm or contradict it. The evidence points toward continuation of the earnings engine and a reversion of price toward intrinsic value as capex panic fades. The caution is timing.

PERFORMANCE (6 MONTHS)

Over the past six months IBIT swung through a wide range and finished with a modest net loss. It bottomed at the end of June, rallied strongly into a late-September high, and has since given back a meaningful part of that advance. The latest month was the sharpest leg down. Price now sits in the upper half of the range, not at either extreme. The shape reads as a failed breakout, not a clean trend in either direction.

DRIVERS

The Fed path is the dominant macro driver. The market prices a high likelihood of a rate hike at the December meeting, which keeps real yields elevated and financial conditions restrictive. That is a poor backdrop for a liquidity-sensitive asset with no earnings or cash flow to anchor its valuation. Inflation and yield pressure sit behind the hike pricing and are the channel through which the macro regime weighs on the asset.

The single piece of video evidence points the same way. In early July, Adam Khoo said he may agree with Grantham that Bitcoin goes to zero, and he treats it as a speculative, mania-chasing vehicle. The remark is old and its weight has decayed, so it adds a valuation-skeptic voice but little new information.

CONTINUE OR REVERT

The call is bearish: continuation of weakness over reversion. The evidence-adjusted posterior is essentially unchanged from the market-implied starting point, so the Khoo remark confirms the crowd's pricing and does not fight it. The odds, the recent price action and the evidence all lean the same way, so there is no conflict to resolve. I weight the market odds more heavily because they reflect live Fed pricing, while the video item is a stale, single-voice opinion. Conviction is high because the signals agree, not because the evidence is strong.

The rebound from the June low was real, but a good part of it has already reversed, which fits a failed breakout inside a restrictive-rate regime. The main thing that would change the view is a repricing of the Fed. If December hike odds fell decisively below even, the rate headwind would ease and the signal would be revised toward neutral or better. A sustained move back through the September high would also show buyers overriding the macro backdrop. Absent either, the path of least resistance stays lower.
